Sophos

A cognitive operating system.

Ontology
What exists. What a system is permitted to treat as real, and who decides that.
Epistemology
What is known. On what grounds a claim is admitted, and how it is justified.
Teleology
What is intended. Which ends are being served, and by whose authority.

Sophos governs all three. It is in private development, and we are not publishing the architecture yet.

The work

A system for how a mind reasons, not merely what it answers.

Sophos is being built for the institutions that will have to live with machines that outthink them. The difficulty is not capability. It is authority: who decides, on what grounds, what is recorded when a decision matters, and what happens when the answer and the reasoning disagree.

That question is unglamorous, and almost nobody is answering it. We are.

We are not taking clients yet. We are building the thing.
