Sophos

σοφός · wise

A cognitive operating system.

Ontology
What exists. What a system is permitted to treat as real, and who decides that.
Epistemology
What is known. On what grounds a claim is admitted, and how it is justified.
Teleology
What is intended. Which ends are being served, and by whose authority.

Sophos governs all three. It is in private development, and we are not publishing the architecture yet.

The work

A system for how a mind reasons.

Institutions are starting to hand real decisions to AI systems. When one of those systems gets something badly wrong, the mistake is seldom in the reasoning. It happens earlier, in how the question was read. The system settles on one meaning of an ambiguous request, or quietly swaps the goal it was handed for an easier one, and then reasons carefully about the wrong problem. Ask whether to walk or drive to the car wash, and it may tell you to walk because the place is close, forgetting that the truck is the thing that needs washing. The answer reads as sound. Nothing in it shows where it went wrong.

Better instructions in the prompt do not reach this, and neither does a larger model. Both improve the reasoning, and the reasoning is seldom where it broke. What is missing is anything standing between the system and the answer it gives: a check that its evidence can carry the claim it makes, and a way to say “there is not enough here to answer” that counts as a proper result rather than a failure. Sophos supplies that.
