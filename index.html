How we work

Careful is faster

The expensive failures are rarely the clever ones. They are assumptions that went unchecked. Our method is built to catch those early, while they are still cheap.

Measure before decidingClaims about a system are checked against the system, not against what was expected of it.
Make it reversibleWork is structured so a wrong turn can be undone without a rescue mission.
Write down the limitsWhat a tool does not cover is documented as plainly as what it does.
Verify the whole thingParts that pass alone can still fail together, so we test the assembly, not only the components.

Who you are working with

A small shop, deliberately

We keep the work close to the people doing it. That means fewer handoffs, plainer answers, and a direct line to whoever is actually building your system. When something sits outside our depth, we will say so and point you to someone better suited. That answer is worth more to you than a contract.
